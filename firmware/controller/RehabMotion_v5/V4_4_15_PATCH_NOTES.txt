RehabMotion V4.4.16 COMPILE FIX

This revision fixes a V4.4.14 migration error in torso_reference.h.
TRFrontSnapshot was correctly changed from {a,b,d} to {a,b,e}, but three old call sites still referenced s.d.
Fixed:
- trCaptureNeutral(): s.d -> s.e for E gravity and E quaternion.
- trFrontGeometry(): s.d -> s.e for E torso gravity.
- Geometry/local variable names and serial labels updated from D/gd/D_tilt to E/ge/E_tilt where they represented the torso sensor.

No training thresholds, ROM math, semantic K11 math, or ABCDE role mapping were changed by this compile-fix revision.
A=left upper, B=left lower, C=right upper, D=right lower, E=waist/abdomen.
